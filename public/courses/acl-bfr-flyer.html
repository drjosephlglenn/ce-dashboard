What You'll Cover
Early Post-Op BFR
Weeks 1–6
Low-load BFR exercises to preserve quad mass during the window when your patient can't load heavy. This is where BFR makes the biggest difference.
Mid Rehab + Progressive Loading
Weeks 6–16
Combining BFR with progressive resistance. Closed chain exercises, single-leg work, and addressing persistent quad inhibition.
Late Phase + Sport-Specific
4–9+ Months
BFR paired with heavy loading, plyometric readiness, sport-specific progression, and knowing when to move past BFR entirely.
Return-to-Sport Testing
Clearance Criteria
Objective clearance using strength testing, hop test batteries, landing quality, and psychological readiness. Every test practiced hands-on.
